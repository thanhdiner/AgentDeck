# Prompt: Market and Reference Research

You are a senior product researcher and technical product strategist.

I want to build a local desktop app similar in workflow to AI coding cockpit tools: multi-pane terminals, workspace sidebar, tasks, agent sessions, logs, and review panels.

Research and define the product direction.

Output in English.

Cover:

1. Product category
2. Similar tools and what they do well
3. Similar tools and what they do badly
4. Target user
5. Main use cases
6. What must be in MVP
7. What must be delayed
8. Risks if scope gets too large
9. Final recommended MVP scope

Hard rule:

Keep the scope practical. I am building this as a solo developer. Do not suggest enterprise features for phase 1.
